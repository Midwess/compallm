# Organization settings

Organization settings are scoped to the active workspace. Settings persistence and deletion remain planned.

Organization identity includes its name and proposed logo and URL identifier. Fiscal-month, deployment-region, and onboarding settings require concrete business requirements before becoming configurable application behavior.

Organization deletion requires explicit confirmation naming the organization. Authorization, last-owner handling, retention, recovery, shared-resource effects, and the deletion process remain unresolved. A confirmation action alone does not delete data.

See [organization membership](adr.md) and [account lifecycle](../portal/account.md).
